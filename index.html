I’m Rebecca Woody, an astrophysicist at the Center for Astrophysics, Harvard & Smithsonian. My research focuses on stellar evolution and on quantifying the uncertainties that go into stellar ages.

A central thread of my work is a program to improve stellar age and evolution uncertainties by combining posterior samples for individual, well-characterized stars with generalized MIST grids. Along the way I build up a small catalog of benchmark stars — each with its own visual identity — such as AI Phe, μ Cas, and ν Ind.
