85 Pegasi is a nearby (~39 light-year) old, metal-poor system ([Fe/H] ≈ −0.5).
The primary is a G-type dwarf slightly less massive than the Sun, orbited by a
cooler, lower-mass companion.

## Why it's here

Because 85 Peg is both astrometric and spectroscopic, its component masses can
be pinned down dynamically. A metal-poor binary with measured masses is a
valuable calibration point for low-mass stellar structure and for the
mass–luminosity relation away from solar composition.
