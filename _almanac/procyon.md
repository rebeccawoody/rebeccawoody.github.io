Procyon (α Canis Minoris) is a bright, nearby (~11.5 light-year) F5 IV–V star
that is beginning to evolve off the main sequence. It has a faint white-dwarf
companion, Procyon B.

## Why it's here

Procyon has both a well-measured interferometric diameter and detected
solar-like oscillations, and its mass is constrained by the binary orbit. As a
slightly-evolved star more massive than the Sun, it is an important test of how
convection and the mixing length behave outside the narrow solar regime.
