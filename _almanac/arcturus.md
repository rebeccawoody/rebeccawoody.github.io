Arcturus (α Boötis) is a nearby (~37 light-year) K1.5 III red giant. It is
mildly metal-poor ([Fe/H] ≈ −0.5) and moves through the Galaxy with a high
space velocity as part of the Arcturus stream, marking it as an older-population
star.

## Why it's here

Arcturus is a long-standing spectral standard — the reference *Arcturus atlas*
is a staple of stellar spectroscopy — and its angular diameter is
well measured. As a bright, well-characterized cool giant it anchors models of
convection and mixing length in evolved, low-mass stars.
