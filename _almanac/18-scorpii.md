18 Scorpii is a nearby (~46 light-year) G2 V star that is remarkably similar to
the Sun in mass, temperature, and chemical composition — one of the closest and
most complete "solar twins" known.

## Why it's here

A star this close to a true solar analog is invaluable as a reference: it lets
us test how well we understand the Sun's own structure and evolution by
comparing against a nearly identical, independently observed star.
