55 Cancri is a nearby (~41 light-year) binary. The primary, 55 Cancri A, is a
metal-rich K-type dwarf that hosts a rich system of planets — including the hot
super-Earth 55 Cancri e — while a distant red dwarf, 55 Cancri B, is bound to
it at wide separation.

## Why it's here

As a bright, metal-rich planet host, 55 Cnc A is a well-studied case where
accurate stellar parameters directly feed the characterization of its planets.
It is a useful benchmark for connecting stellar models to exoplanet science at
above-solar metallicity.
