α Centauri is the nearest stellar system to the Sun (~4.37 light-years), a
triple made up of α Cen A (a G2 V star much like the Sun), α Cen B (a cooler
K1 V dwarf), and Proxima Centauri (a distant M-dwarf that hosts the planet
Proxima b).

## Why it's here

Few stars are as thoroughly characterized as α Cen A and B: their orbit yields
precise dynamical masses, both show solar-like oscillations, and their angular
diameters have been measured interferometrically. Together they form one of the
most demanding benchmarks for stellar-structure and mixing-length modeling on
either side of the Sun's mass.
