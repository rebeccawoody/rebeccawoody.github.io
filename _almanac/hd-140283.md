HD 140283, nicknamed the "Methuselah Star," is an extremely metal-poor
([Fe/H] ≈ −2.4) subgiant roughly 200 light-years away. Age estimates place it
among the oldest stars known, close to the age of the Universe itself.

## Why it's here

HD 140283 has a precise parallax and a measured angular diameter, so its radius
and luminosity are tightly constrained even at its extreme, near-primordial
composition. That makes it a cornerstone for testing stellar-interior models —
and the absolute age scale of the oldest populations — where the mixing length
at very low metallicity has direct consequences.
