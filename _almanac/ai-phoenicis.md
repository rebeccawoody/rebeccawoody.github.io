AI Phoenicis is a bright, detached, double-lined eclipsing binary with a long
orbital period (~24.6 days). It pairs a G-type star that has evolved onto the
subgiant branch with a slightly cooler, less-evolved companion of nearly equal
mass.

## Why it's here

Because AI Phe is both eclipsing and double-lined, its component masses and
radii can be measured to a fraction of a percent — among the best-determined
of any binary. That precision, combined with two coeval stars caught at
different evolutionary stages, makes it a stringent benchmark for stellar
models, mixing-length calibration, and stellar ages.
