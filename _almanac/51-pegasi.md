51 Pegasi is a nearby (~50 light-year) Sun-like star, slightly more evolved and
slightly metal-rich than the Sun. In 1995 it became the first main-sequence
star found to host an exoplanet — the hot Jupiter 51 Pegasi b (Dimidium) — a
discovery that opened the field of exoplanet science.

## Why it's here

Beyond its historical importance, 51 Peg is a well-characterized solar analog,
useful as a reference point for solar-type stellar models and for placing the
Sun in the context of similar, slightly-evolved stars.
