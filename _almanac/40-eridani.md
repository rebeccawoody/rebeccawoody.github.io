40 Eridani (also called Keid) is a nearby (~16 light-year) triple system. Its
components are 40 Eri A, an orange K-type dwarf visible to the naked eye; 40 Eri
B, a white dwarf; and 40 Eri C, a small red-dwarf flare star.

## Why it's here

40 Eri B is the most easily observed white dwarf in the sky — bright and well
separated from its primary, it is a favorite target for small telescopes and a
tangible way to see a stellar remnant with your own eyes. The system also has a
place in popular culture as the fictional home of Vulcan.
