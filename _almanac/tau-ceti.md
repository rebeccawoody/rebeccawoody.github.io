τ Ceti is one of the nearest single, Sun-like stars (~12 light-years), a quiet
G8 V dwarf that is slightly metal-poor ([Fe/H] ≈ −0.5). It hosts a debris disk
and a set of candidate planets.

## Why it's here

Its proximity and brightness allow a direct interferometric measurement of its
angular diameter, and hence its radius. Well-characterized single solar-type
stars like τ Ceti provide clean tests of stellar models and of the assumptions
that go into modeling convection in Sun-like envelopes.
