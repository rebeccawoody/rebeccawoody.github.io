title: Classical Cases of Non-Solar Mixing Length in Low Mass Stars
description: Using geometric radii of detached eclipsing binaries and stars with diameters measured interferometrically we constrained the mixing length, testing literature prescriptions that go beyond a fixed, solar-calibrated mixing length parameter.  Beyond megnetically inflated fast rotators we found no population level trends for the mixing lengths in eclipsing binaries, calling into question the existence of a ubiquitous calibration beyond the Sun.  The mixing length in the interferometric sample on the other hand displayed a strong dependence on metallicity, where very metal poor stars reaching very subsolar mixing lengths.  Such a strong metallicity dependence has implications for the absolute age scale and age-metallicity relation observed in the Galaxy's oldest populations.
