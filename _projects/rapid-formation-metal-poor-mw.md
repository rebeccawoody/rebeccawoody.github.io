title: The Rapid Formation of the Metal-poor Milky Way
description: Using isochrone ages for main sequence turn off and subgiant stars observed in the H3 survey we measured the end of star formation in accreted dwarf galaxies that built up the metal poor populations in the Milky Way.  With these star formation truncation times we infered the assembly history of the Galactic halo, finding a rapid assembly  with the GSE merger 10 Gyr ago marking the last major disruptive merger that our Galaxy has experienced.
